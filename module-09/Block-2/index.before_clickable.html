Comprehension Check

  1. Why did Varya's nose become unusual?
  2. How did other children treat her at school?
  3. Who was Sergey Lukyanov and how did he help?
  4. What was risky about the operation?
  5. How did Varya react when she first saw herself after the operation?
  6. What changed when she returned to school?
  7. What do the comments on YouTube show?
  8. According to the text, what made the happy ending possible?

Think & Analyse

  1. The text says: "The machine saved her life, but it also changed it." How can technology have both positive and negative effects?
  2. Dr. Friedlander "had given up his weekend for this girl." Why do doctors sometimes go beyond their duty?
  3. Varya whispered "Thank you" to the lake, not to Sergey or her mother. What do you think she was feeling?
  4. The caption under the video said: "She made it. Now she's seeing the world." What does "seeing the world" mean here?

Discuss & Relate

  1. Have you ever seen someone being teased because of their appearance? What did you do? What could you have done?
  2. Social media is often criticized, but here it helped change a life. Can you think of other examples where the internet was used for good?
  3. Varya's story shows that strangers can become a kind of family. Why do you think people are willing to help someone they've never met?

Grammar in Context · Passive Voice

Exercise 1 · Identify

a) “She had to be put on a ventilator.” — Modal + Passive Infinitive
b) “The operation was too risky.” — Not passive
c) “Money was raised.” — Tense: ______
d) “Her nose had to be reconstructed.” — Modal + Passive Infinitive
e) “The bandages were going to be removed.” — Future in the past passive
f) “Varya was given a mirror.” — Tense: ______ (Double object passive)

Exercise 2 · Active → Passive

a) Someone put Varya on a ventilator.
→ Varya ______ ______ on a ventilator.

b) The doctors reconstructed her nose layer by layer.
→ Her nose ______ ______ layer by layer.

c) A nurse gently unwrapped the bandages.
→ The bandages ______ ______ gently by a nurse.

d) Thousands of strangers donated money.
→ Money ______ ______ by thousands of strangers.

Exercise 3 · Complete

a) The operation ______ (perform) in a clinic in Irkutsk.
b) Varya ______ (keep) in the hospital for several weeks.
c) A mirror ______ (give) to her so she could see herself.
d) The video ______ (watch) by millions of people.
