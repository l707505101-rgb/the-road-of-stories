A LOVE STORY IN DETAILS

Masha and Artyom are very young. On their wedding day, he was 19, and she was 18. Many people might say they are too young for such a serious step. But they were happy and confident in their decision. They wanted to unite their destinies right now — while their elderly grandparents are still alive and healthy, so they could share this beautiful celebration with them.

Planning with Heart

When preparing for the wedding, the young couple studied the history of their families very carefully. They wanted these stories to become the foundation of the speeches and the entire ceremony. The couple thought about everything down to the smallest detail — the interior, the colour schemes of the hall and the guests' clothes, the beautiful accessories, the position of every single flower, and even the musical repertoire for each piece.

When it came time to choose who would bring the rings, they initially thought of following tradition and asked their younger sisters. But then they realized: their sisters still have many such events ahead of them, including their own weddings. So they made a different choice. They decided to ask their grandmothers. This was a gesture of deep respect for the oldest representatives of both families. It was their way of saying thank you for their love and tenderness, for being a true example of femininity and the ability to keep the family hearth warm. Masha's grandparents have been together for 45 years, and Artyom's — for 47 years.

A Vision of Family

The wedding organizers were initially a little nervous. They had never met a young couple who paid such careful attention to every detail of their celebration. Masha and Artyom chose as the leitmotif of their wedding the strength of family bonds, loyalty, and faithfulness — as shown in European films, especially Italian ones. At first, they didn't have a clear vision. But after watching a huge amount of material and talking to specialists, they found their inspiration: the concept of the film The Godfather. The main idea became «Family above all.» They wanted to make the celebration truly original and personal — and they succeeded.

As the organizers later admitted, they had never had such an experience before. The depth with which the young couple immersed themselves in every detail was a revelation. Now, strangers sometimes even ask Masha and Artyom for advice on organizing their own weddings. This fact speaks volumes.

Learning to Agree

During the preparations, Masha and Artyom sometimes had different visions of certain aspects. But even then, they proved that two loving hearts can always find a compromise while remaining different individuals. This beautiful young couple is now a family. They tested their feelings for five years. Their love began when they were both still schoolchildren. Their feelings were tested by trials, as often happens. Not everyone can withstand such tests. It requires a strong feeling, strong characters, and the ability to yield to each other, to listen, and to negotiate. These skills are not common to everyone, even to people wise with life experience.

Yes, Masha and Artyom's celebration was an unforgettable event for all participants, with an aesthetic worthy of aristocrats. It was a grand celebration with many invited professionals — photographers, reels-makers, musicians, and organizers. But above all, it was an event that showed everyone the supremacy of family over everything else.

A New Beginning

While preparing for the wedding, the young couple, of course, were not on a creative vacation. Masha successfully studied at university, creating amazing creative projects. Artyom worked, showing himself to be a caring and responsible head of the young family. Their organization, responsibility, and desire to make the lives of those they love better gave them wings and the energy to create something that could serve as a wonderful example for many others!

The Honeymoon Adventure

In the summer, the newlyweds went on a month-long journey, which became a beautiful continuation of their new life as a family. A week in Moscow was an opportunity to discover new places, enjoy romantic walks, interesting excursions, and moments of reflection. The next stop was Georgia — Batumi and Tbilisi.

But between their stay in Moscow and their arrival in Georgia, an event occurred that greatly frightened the young couple. Their flight took place in stormy weather. Lightning flashed outside the window, and thunder roared. The plane began to pitch violently at times. People around them were screaming and praying; many, seriously frightened, mentally said goodbye to their loved ones. But everything ended well — the landing was soft.

The author believes that nothing in life happens for no reason. This incident showed Masha and Artyom that they are only at the beginning of their happy journey, that anything is possible in life — different stripes, different events. In just a few months of the year their family was born, they had already experienced several beautiful, bright events — and found themselves together in a place where it was frightening, where it seemed everything was ending. But Life, or the World, loves bright, kind, creative, and interesting people. The World, I think, has big plans for these people. They have a lot to do — to see the world, to decorate it with their beautiful deeds, to give themselves and the world children, to do something good in this life. That's why this adventure ended well.

The Beauty of the World

Batumi amazed the young people with its breathtaking beauty and warmth — the warmth of the sea and the people who inhabit it. Communicating with tourists from different countries, with hospitable hosts, visiting a park of musicians in a neighbouring town, a botanical garden, seeing the world-famous statue of Ali and Nino, trying local cuisine, and talking with residents — all this became an unforgettable adventure. And then there were Tbilisi, Vladikavkaz, and the fairy-tale Kislovodsk. So many places and unforgettable impressions that not everyone manages to get in a lifetime. And this is only the beginning!

The Lesson

We wish this beautiful young couple faithful, beautiful love, and good luck on their life's journey. May their example inspire others, showing them that everything that seems unlikely at first can become real.
