▾LEARNING · EXPLORE THE STORY

FOR THE HEART AND MIND · A. COMPREHENSION CHECK

  1. Where did Erling Haaland grow up? What sports did he try as a child?
  2. How was his path to success different from other football stars?
  3. What does Haaland say about himself to stay humble?
  4. What is the «Viking Row» ritual and who invented it?
  5. How did Haaland react when a young fan cried after Norway's defeat?
  6. What did Haaland say about his own unexpected success?
  7. What does Haaland do in his free time to stay balanced?
  8. What is the story about the book he donated to his hometown?

B. THINK & ANALYSE

  1. Do you think his parents' freedom helped him become happier and more successful? Why?
  2. Why do simple, shared rituals create such powerful bonds?
  3. What does «We just need to enjoy the moment, because nothing is forever» teach us about pressure?
  4. Why do people love Haaland not just for his goals, but for his personality?
  5. Why does the «cyborg» / «humble guy» contrast make him more popular?
  6. What does donating a very expensive book tell us about his character and values?

C. DISCUSS & RELATE

  1. What is your «football» — an activity that brings you pure joy?
  2. Is it possible to be a «superstar» and remain «ordinary»?
  3. What does «Success is not just about winning. It's about staying human» mean to you?
  4. Have you ever seen a meme about a famous person that was funny but also kind?

EXERCISES · THE VIKING ROAD

FOR THE HEART AND MIND

A. First Impressions — Stepping into His Shoes

Close your eyes, listen to the song, and then answer:

  1. What is the main feeling Haaland expresses in this song — is it pride, humility, surprise, or something else?
  2. The song says: «I never thought I'd have it all.» Why do you think he was surprised by his own success?
  3. If you were Haaland, which line from this song would you want people to remember most about you? Why?

B. The Man Behind the Mask

Read the lines from the song and reflect on what they reveal about Haaland's character:

Line from the SongWhat does this tell us about Haaland?
«They didn't push, they let me choose»He values freedom and choice. He is grateful to his parents.
«I carry joy, not just the weight»

«My roots are strong, I'm not bereft»_

C. Compare and Discuss — A Mirror of Your Own Life

These questions are designed to connect Haaland's story to your own journey.

  1. The song begins: «I grew up in a town so small.» Haaland is proud of his small-town roots. What is one thing about your background (where you're from, your family, your early life) that you carry with you with pride? Why?
  2. He says: «They didn't push, they let me choose.» Do you feel you have the freedom to choose your own path? Who in your life «pushes» you and who «lets you choose»?
  3. He admits: «I never thought I'd have it all.» Haaland was surprised by his own success. Is there something in your life that turned out better than you expected? What was it?
  4. The chorus says: «I'm just a Viking on the road.» If you had to describe your own life as a journey, what would you be — a «Viking on the road,» a «sailor at sea,» a «pilgrim on a path»? Why?

D. The Gift of Roots

  1. In the bridge, Haaland sings about giving a rare book to his hometown: «I gave a book to my hometown / To show them that I never left.» Why is this gesture so powerful? What does it show about his values?
  2. If you could give one meaningful gift to your hometown or your school, what would it be — and why?
  3. The word «bereft» means «deprived, left without something.» He says «I'm not bereft» — he is not empty, he has roots. What does it mean to have «roots»? What gives you strength when you feel lost?
