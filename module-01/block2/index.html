READ · THE UNSAID

Sophie and Jake had been best friends since sixth grade. They worked on every school project together. So when the history teacher announced a big presentation, they immediately teamed up.

Sophie was a perfectionist. She spent hours on her part, researching facts and creating beautiful slides. On Tuesday night, at 11:47 PM, she sent Jake her draft with a message: «Hey, I've finished my part. Could you look at it and tell me what you think?»

Jake was exhausted. He had practised football for three hours, then helped his little brother with homework. He glanced at the message, opened the file, skimmed it, and tapped a quick «like» emoji. Then his eyes closed.

The next day, they met in the library. Sophie was cold. She answered his questions with one word: «Fine.» «Okay.» «Sure.» She didn't look at him. Her arms were crossed.

Jake felt a familiar anger rising. What's her problem? I said her work was good. Why is she acting like this? He stopped talking too. An awkward silence grew between them.

Their friend Mark sat nearby, watching. Finally, he turned to Sophie and asked quietly: «Hey, is everything okay? How's your grandmother? I heard she was taken to the hospital on Tuesday.»

Sophie's eyes filled with tears. «She's stable now, but… I was so scared. I didn't sleep that night. I just worked to distract myself.»

Jake froze. Tuesday night. While he was sleeping, Sophie had been alone, terrified, waiting for news. She didn't need a grammar check. She needed a friend.

He took a deep breath. «Sophie… I didn't know. I'm so sorry. I was blind.»

Sophie looked up. «I didn't tell you. I thought you would notice something was wrong. But you just sent an emoji.»

«I was exhausted,» Jake said. «But that's not an excuse. Tell me now. How are you? How is she?»

For the first time that day, Sophie's shoulders relaxed. She started talking. Jake listened. And the unsaid was finally spoken.

♥ FOR THE HEART AND MIND
A. COMPREHENSION CHECK
  1. How long had Sophie and Jake been friends?
  2. Why did Sophie send Jake a message late at night?
  3. How did Jake respond to her message?
  4. How did Sophie behave the next day? Give two examples.
  5. What did Mark ask Sophie?
  6. What had happened to Sophie's grandmother?
  7. How did Jake feel when he understood the truth?
  8. What did Jake say to Sophie at the end?
B. THINK & ANALYSE
  1. Why did Sophie act coldly towards Jake? Was it only because of the emoji?
  2. Jake said: «I was exhausted. But that's not an excuse.» Why is it important that he added the second part?
  3. The story is called «The Unsaid». What things remained unsaid between Sophie and Jake? Why do people often leave important things unsaid?
  4. Do you think Sophie was fair to Jake? Why or why not?
C. DISCUSS & RELATE
  1. Have you ever misunderstood a friend's behaviour because you didn't know their situation? What happened?
  2. Is it our responsibility to notice when a friend is hurting, or should they tell us directly?
  3. What helps you repair a relationship after a conflict?

B. GRAMMAR IN CONTEXT

1. Past Perfect vs Past Simple

Find the sentence: «While he was sleeping, Sophie had been alone, terrified, waiting for news.»

Why is Past Perfect used here? (Because her being alone happened BEFORE his realisation.)

Complete: When Jake understood everything, Sophie ______ (already / spend) a sleepless night.

2. Direct speech punctuation

Look at the dialogue. How are the speakers' words formatted?

Rewrite one exchange as indirect speech.

Example: Sophie said that her grandmother was stable but she had been very scared.

C. BRAIN WORKOUT

1. Скорость реакции (быстрое преобразование):

Учитель называет слово из глоссария, ученики должны как можно быстрее назвать его антоним или противоположную ситуацию.

  • exhausted → energetic
  • cold → warm
  • awkward → comfortable / natural
  • to relax → to tense up

2. Эпизодическая память + визуализация (метод локусов):

Закрой глаза. Представь свою комнату. Мысленно «размести» в разных её углах 5 новых слов из текста (например, draft на письменном столе, awkward на стуле, silence под кроватью и т.д.). Открой глаза и «пройди» по комнате, называя слова.

3. Восстанови последовательность:

Расставь события в правильном порядке (1–6):

  1. ___ Jake realised what had happened to Sophie's grandmother.
  2. ___ Sophie sent Jake her draft.
  3. ___ Mark asked Sophie about her grandmother.
  4. ___ Sophie and Jake teamed up for the project.
  5. ___ Sophie was cold and avoided eye contact.
  6. ___ Jake apologised and asked Sophie to tell him everything.
