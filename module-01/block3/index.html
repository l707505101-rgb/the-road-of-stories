READ & LISTEN · VOICES ACROSS TIME AND CULTURES

VOICE 1. Ancient Greece — Aristotle

«Friendship is a single soul dwelling in two bodies. It is not about being together all the time, but about being connected even when you are apart. A true friend understands your silence, not only your words. The desire for friendship comes quickly, but friendship itself is a slow-growing fruit. It must be watered with trust and ripened in the sun of shared experience.»

TRANSCRIPT

«Friendship is a single soul dwelling in two bodies. It is not about being together all the time, but about being connected even when you are apart. A true friend understands your silence, not only your words. The desire for friendship comes quickly, but friendship itself is a slow-growing fruit. It must be watered with trust and ripened in the sun of shared experience.»

VOICE 2. Africa — Yoruba proverb, Nigeria

«Hold a true friend with both hands. Do not let go easily, because such a bond is rare. A true friend knows the song of your heart. And when you lose your way, they can sing it back to you until you remember who you are.»

TRANSCRIPT

«Hold a true friend with both hands. Do not let go easily, because such a bond is rare. A true friend knows the song of your heart. And when you lose your way, they can sing it back to you until you remember who you are.»

VOICE 3. Modern Psychology — Dr. Brené Brown

«Friendship is not about being perfect for each other. It is about being real with each other. Vulnerability is the birthplace of connection. You cannot have a strong tie without the courage to say: 'I'm struggling. I need you.' And the other person saying: 'I see you. I'm here.' Being real is harder than being perfect, but it is the only way to truly connect.»

TRANSCRIPT

«Friendship is not about being perfect for each other. It is about being real with each other. Vulnerability is the birthplace of connection. You cannot have a strong tie without the courage to say: 'I'm struggling. I need you.' And the other person saying: 'I see you. I'm here.' Being real is harder than being perfect, but it is the only way to truly connect.»

VOICE 4. Japan — Bushido

«A true friend is one who walks in when the rest of the world walks out. They do not give you what you want; they give you what you need — honesty, even when it hurts. Loyalty is not blind agreement. Loyalty is holding your friend to a higher standard because you believe in who they can become. A friend who challenges you is often the one who cares the most.»

TRANSCRIPT

«A true friend is one who walks in when the rest of the world walks out. They do not give you what you want; they give you what you need — honesty, even when it hurts. Loyalty is not blind agreement. Loyalty is holding your friend to a higher standard because you believe in who they can become. A friend who challenges you is often the one who cares the most.»

♥ FOR THE HEART AND MIND
A. COMPREHENSION CHECK
  1. According to Aristotle, what is friendship?
  2. Why does Aristotle compare friendship to a «slow-growing fruit»?
  3. What does the Yoruba proverb mean: «Hold a true friend with both hands»?
  4. What does Dr. Brené Brown say is the «birthplace of connection»?
  5. According to the Samurai Code, what does a true friend do when the world walks out?
  6. What is the difference between «giving what you want» and «giving what you need»?
B. THINK & ANALYSE
  1. Aristotle says a friend is someone who understands «your silence, not only your words». How does this connect to Text 1.B — «The Unsaid»?
  2. The African proverb says a friend «knows the song of your heart». What is your «heart's song»? Does anyone know it?
  3. Dr. Brown says vulnerability is «the birthplace of connection». But in Text 1.A, Liam was afraid to be vulnerable. Why is vulnerability so difficult — and why is it necessary?
  4. The Samurai Code says: «Loyalty is not blind agreement. Loyalty is holding your friend to a higher standard.» Can you think of a situation where a friend showed you loyalty by disagreeing with you?
C. DISCUSS & RELATE
  1. Which of these four «voices» is closest to your own understanding of friendship? Why?
  2. Do you think social media helps or hurts true friendship? Use ideas from the texts to support your opinion.
  3. Is it possible to have a strong friendship without vulnerability? Why?
  4. In your culture, are there proverbs or sayings about friendship? Share one with the class (in English or with translation).

A FRIEND'S CODE

КОД ДРУГА

Voice 1 — Aristotle

"Friendship is a single soul dwelling in two bodies.

It is not about being together all the time,

But about being connected even when you are apart.

A true friend understands your silence, not only your words.

The desire for friendship comes quickly,

But friendship itself is a slow-growing fruit.

It must be watered with trust

And ripened in the sun of shared experience."

Голос 1 — Аристотель

«Дружба — это одна душа, живущая в двух телах.

Она не в том, чтобы быть вместе всё время,

А в том, чтобы быть связанными, даже когда вы далеко.

Настоящий друг понимает твоё молчание, не только твои слова.

Желание дружбы приходит быстро,

Но сама дружба — это медленно зреющий плод.

Её нужно поливать доверием

И дать ей созреть на солнце общего опыта».

Voice 2 — Yoruba proverb

"Hold a true friend with both hands.

Do not let go easily, because such a bond is rare.

A true friend knows the song of your heart.

And when you lose your way,

They can sing it back to you

Until you remember who you are."

Голос 2 — Пословица йоруба

«Держи настоящего друга обеими руками.

Не отпускай легко, потому что такая связь редка.

Настоящий друг знает песню твоего сердца.

И когда ты сбиваешься с пути,

Они могут спеть её тебе,

Пока ты не вспомнишь, кто ты есть».

Voice 3 — Brené Brown

"Friendship is not about being perfect for each other.

It is about being real with each other.

Vulnerability is the birthplace of connection.

You cannot have a strong tie without the courage to say:

'I'm struggling. I need you.'

And the other person saying:

'I see you. I'm here.'

Being real is harder than being perfect,

But it is the only way to truly connect."

Голос 3 — Брене Браун

«Дружба — это не быть идеальными друг для друга.

Это быть настоящими друг с другом.

Уязвимость — это колыбель связи.

Крепкая связь невозможна без смелости сказать:

«Мне трудно. Ты мне нужен».

И услышать в ответ:

«Я вижу тебя. Я здесь».

Быть настоящим труднее, чем быть идеальным,

Но это единственный способ по-настоящему соединиться».

Voice 4 — Bushido

"A true friend is one who walks in

When the rest of the world walks out.

They do not give you what you want;

They give you what you need —

Honesty, even when it hurts.

Loyalty is not blind agreement.

Loyalty is holding your friend to a higher standard

Because you believe in who they can become.

A friend who challenges you

Is often the one who cares the most."

Голос 4 — Бусидо

«Настоящий друг — это тот, кто входит,

Когда весь остальной мир выходит.

Они дают тебе не то, что ты хочешь;

Они дают тебе то, что тебе нужно —

Честность, даже когда она ранит.

Верность — это не слепое согласие.

Верность — это держать своего друга на высокой планке,

Потому что ты веришь в того, кем он может стать.

Друг, который бросает тебе вызов,

Часто заботится больше всех».

Final

"To me, real friendship means

Being seen, being heard,

And being loved — not for who you pretend to be,

But for who you truly are."

Финал

«Для меня настоящая дружба —

Это быть увиденным, быть услышанным

И быть любимым — не за того, кем ты притворяешься,

А за того, кто ты есть на самом деле».
