01 · The Story Window · Reading
Read for the main idea: What does Leo discover about the difference between buying something and feeling rich?

Leo wanted one thing: the new Pro-Gamer X-3000 console. His friends talked about it every day. The screen was 4K. The sound was like in a real cinema. Leo had to have it.

It cost 500 dollars.

Leo didn't have 500 dollars. He had 20 dollars from his grandmother. So he started saving. No snacks after school. No comics. No apps. Every dollar went into a blue box on his shelf.

It took him eight months.

The day he opened the box, his hands were shaking. He counted the money. Twice. Three times. Five hundred and twelve dollars. More than enough.

He ran to the shop. He bought the console. He ran home. He connected it to the TV. He put the first disc in.

And then he just… sat there.

The graphics were perfect. The sound was perfect. But Leo felt nothing. He played for an hour. Then he turned it off.

Why do I feel so empty? he thought.

He looked at the blue box on his shelf. It was empty now. But then he remembered: eight months of small choices. Eight months of saying “no” to little things so he could say “yes” to one big thing.

He remembered something else too.

His grandmother. Every Sunday, he visited her. They baked cookies together. She told stories about her childhood. He laughed at her old jokes. She never talked about money. She just liked being with him.

Leo closed his eyes. The console was shiny and new. But the real treasure wasn't in his hands. It was in those Sunday afternoons he almost missed because he was too busy saving.

He picked up his phone.

“Grandma? Can I come over tomorrow? I miss your cookies.”

Her voice was warm. “I was just thinking about you, my boy.”

Leo smiled. And for the first time in eight months, he felt rich.
