Petya, Iris, and the Home on Wheels

Imagine your home on wheels, your office by the ocean, and your neighbours from all over the world. This is the life of Petya and Iris, a couple who have turned their passion for travel into a unique way of living and helping others.

Petya, originally from Odesa, Ukraine, moved to China for work. There, he met Iris, a local Chinese girl. Despite the cultural differences and her parents' initial worries about a foreigner, their love grew strong. They got married and soon started a life that many only dream of. They bought a camper van and began traveling across China.

But they didn’t just want to show beautiful postcard views. Their goal was much deeper. On their YouTube channel and Telegram blog, they show life as it really is — the good, the difficult, and the surprising.

Their travels have taken them not only across all of China but also to England, Bali, Italy, Iceland, Bangladesh, Africa, and even Antarctica. Wherever they go, they have one important rule: respect the local traditions and people.

More Than Just Tourists

What makes Petya and Iris special is not just their adventurous spirit but their kind hearts. They don’t just film beautiful places. They talk to local people. They listen to their stories. And when they see someone who needs help, they act.

Iris is a very emotional person. She often cries when she sees someone suffering. And her tears turn into action. The couple is known for helping those in need. Sometimes they simply give money to a poor family. Other times, they buy specific things:

They bought a laptop for a young boy so he could study properly.

They bought a cart for an elderly man so he could carry the goods he had to sell to survive.

In villages, they give sweets and treats to local children, just to see them smile.

They do all this quietly, without loud announcements. Their viewers find out about these good deeds from their videos and posts, which inspires thousands of others to be kinder too.

The Secret of Their Happiness

Many people ask Petya and Iris: "What is the secret of their happy marriage and life?" They answer simply: "We support each other. And we remember that the most important thing is not the places we see, but the people we meet and the hearts we touch.

They live in a small camper van, which can be hard sometimes. There’s not much space. But they have learned that you don't need much to be happy. You need a person who loves you, a sense of adventure, and a desire to make the world a tiny bit better.

Petya and Iris are not just travelers. They are a small force of good, rolling across the planet in their home on wheels, proving that kindness has no nationality and that the best journey is the one you share.

♥ FOR THE HEART AND MIND
A. Comprehension Check
  1. Where is Petya originally from, and where did he meet Iris?
  2. What is special about the way Petya and Iris show their travels?
  3. Why is Iris described as an "emotional person"?
  4. What are three examples of how they help people?
  5. What is the "secret" of their happiness according to them?
B. Think & Analyse
  1. The text says their goal is "deeper" than showing beautiful views. What does this mean?
  2. Why do you think Iris cries when she sees people suffering? Is this a weakness or a strength?
  3. How does their way of helping differ from just giving money to a charity?
  4. What does the phrase "kindness has no nationality" mean?
C. Discuss & Relate
  1. Do you think traveling can make a person more open-minded and kind? Why?
  2. What small acts of kindness could a tourist do when visiting another country?
  3. If you had a YouTube channel, would you use it to show only beautiful things or real life too? Why?

B. Grammar in Context (Passive Voice & "Used to")
1. Find examples of the Past Simple Passive in the text.
Example: A laptop was bought for a young boy.
2. Rewrite the sentences in the Passive Voice:
a) Petya and Iris help many people. → Many people ___________ helped by Petya and Iris.
b) They bought a cart for an elderly man. → A cart ___________ bought for an elderly man.
c) They give sweets to local children. → Sweets ___________ given to local children.
3. "Used to" for past habits:
Petya moved to China. He used to live in Odesa. (He lived there before, but not now.)

Complete the sentence: Iris used to ___________ (live) only in China, but now she travels everywhere with Petya.
C. Brain Workout
1. Phrasal Verbs of Kindness:
• give out (to distribute): They give out sweets to children.
• help out (to assist): They always help out families in need.
• give back (to return help): They believe in giving back to the community.
• care for (to look after): They care for people they meet on their travels.

Task: Write one sentence using each phrasal verb in the context of helping others.
2. Visualizing a Story: Imagine you are a local child who received sweets from Petya and Iris. Describe that moment in 2-3 sentences.
3. The Perfect Gift: Petya and Iris bought a laptop for a student and a cart for an old man. Why were these perfect gifts for those people? What gift would you buy for someone in need in your community?

🎯 GROUP PROJECT — “My Dream, My Kindness”

Тип задания: Творческий коллаж + план действий
Длительность: 3-4 дня

Your Mission: Create a short presentation (poster, digital collage, or video) that answers two questions:

  1. What is one dream you have that you've been waiting to start?
  2. What is one small act of kindness you can do this week?

Use the song “The Home on Wheels” as inspiration.

Format options:

  1. Poster: Split in half. Left side: your dream. Right side: your kindness act.
  2. Digital collage: Use Canva or Google Slides.
  3. Short video (30 seconds): Speak your answers directly to the camera.

Guiding questions for your dream:

  • What's stopping you? (Time? Money? Fear?)
  • What's one small step you can take this week?

Guiding questions for your kindness act:

  • Who needs help?
  • What can you do, even something small?

Final sentence to include:
“The world gives back what you give out.”

🌍 BEYOND THE TEXT

Choose ONE:

  1. A Postcard from the Road: Imagine you are Petya or Iris. Write a short postcard from Antarctica to a friend. Describe one beautiful thing and one difficult thing about living in a camper van there.
  2. A Social Media Post: Write a post for their Telegram channel. Describe one of their acts of kindness (e.g., buying the cart for the old man) as if you were there. Use emojis and a hashtag like #KindnessTravels.
  3. Your Own Travel Vlog Plan: Create a short plan for a travel video. It must include: a) a beautiful place, b) a local person you interview, and c) one small act of kindness you will do.
  4. AI Image Prompt (for the teacher/student): Create a prompt to generate an image of Petya and Iris's camper van in a beautiful, snowy landscape with the northern lights (aurora borealis) in the sky.
    Example prompt: “A cozy white camper van parked on a snowy plain under a dark blue sky filled with green aurora borealis. The van's windows are glowing with warm yellow light. A couple stands outside, looking up at the sky in wonder. Peaceful, magical atmosphere, photorealistic style.”
  5. Discussion: Why is it important that they show “real life” and not just “holiday pictures”? What can we learn from this?
